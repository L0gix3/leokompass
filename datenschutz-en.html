Privacy policy for the “Leokompass” app

Last updated: November 25, 2026 (app version 4.3). This is a translation for your convenience; the German version is the legally binding one.

In short: Leokompass stores your brumation plan and your gecko data only on your phone. The developer does not receive any of your data. There is no user account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking services, and the app itself does not load any content from the internet – all texts are built into the app.

1. What data does the app process?

The app only processes data that you enter yourself. It is stored exclusively locally in the protected storage area of the app on your device:

  • Brumation assistant (per gecko or per brumation group, with the group name you chose): the date of the fecal sample or of the start, the result of the fecal test (pending, negative or positive), the type of brumation (mild or cool), the resting place (box or enclosure) and the type of cooling (cool room or wine cooler), the steps you have ticked off, the checklist, a length of rest you chose, a stop date, the weight-warning limit, and the times and switches of the reminders (steps, checks, airing)
  • optional: “My geckos” – the names of your animals, their sex, birthday (optional, for age and birthday reminder), and the weights and feedings (date, time, number, feeder type – including your own text) you enter (feedings only for the last 3 months; older ones are deleted automatically), and the quarantine schedule with move-in date and ticked-off steps
  • optional: your own photos that you use instead of the cover photos or for your geckos (see section 2)
  • Settings: light or dark theme, language, animations, expert mode, quiz on or off, feeding interval, sounds on or off, fecal sample reminder on or off, date of the last UV tube change, quiz questions you answered wrongly, whether reminders are switched on, and whether the welcome notice, the beginner question and the intro animation have already been shown
  • Widgets: if you place a Leokompass widget on your home screen, it shows your geckos' names and feeding dates or the brumation status – taken directly from this data on the device

The developer has no access to this data. It is not transmitted to him or to third parties.

2. Your own photos

Under “More → Your own photos” you can replace the cover photos of the home page and the topics with your own photos; under “Geckos” you can give each animal a photo. You choose the photos using the Android photo picker – the app only gets access to the photos you select yourself, not to your other pictures. The app stores a reduced copy of them without location and camera data in the protected storage area of the app. These copies are not transmitted, are not included in the automatic Android/Google backup, and are removed when you reset them (“Original”) or uninstall the app. The original in your gallery remains unchanged.

3. Saving a PDF

When you save the brumation plan as a PDF, you choose the location and file name yourself using the Android file picker. The app only gets access to this one file. Where the file goes from there (e.g. a cloud folder, email, a printer) is up to you; the terms of the respective app or service apply.

Backup: under “More → Backup” you can save all the data listed above (including your own photos) into one file and load it back later. You choose the location and file name yourself using the Android file picker; the app only gets access to this one file. The file is not encrypted – keep it the way you want to keep your data. When restoring, the data on the device is replaced by the backup. The app does not transmit anything to the developer or third parties.

Questions and feedback by email: via “Report a bug / idea” or – if the search finds nothing – “Send question by email”, the app opens your email app with a pre-filled text (for the search: your search query). The app itself sends nothing. Only when you send the email yourself do I receive your email address and the content. I use both only to reply and to improve the app, and I delete the email once it is no longer needed for that.

4. Links to websites

The app contains links to the websites of its sources (e.g. German Animal Welfare Federation, RSPCA, ReptiFiles, Wikipedia, www.der-leopardgecko.de; full list under "More" → "Sources"). If you tap one, your browser opens. Only then – as with any visit to a website – is data such as your IP address transmitted to the operator of that website. That operator's privacy policy applies.

6. Google Play

If you install Leokompass via Google Play, Google processes data in accordance with Google's privacy policy. Through the Google Play Console the developer only receives aggregated statistics (e.g. the number of installations) and – if you have agreed to sharing with developers in your Android settings – anonymous crash reports. The developer cannot identify you from these.

Voluntary donations

Under “More” you can make a voluntary donation to the developer (€1.49, €2.50 or €5.00; the price may differ in your currency). The payment is handled exclusively by Google Play. The developer does not receive any payment details; in the Play Console he only sees the order information that Google provides to sellers (e.g. order number, country, amount). A donation does not unlock any features; the app only learns that the payment was successful so that it can say thank you. Donations go exclusively to the developer of the app, not to the operators of the sources named.

7. Storage period and deletion

Your data remains stored until you delete it in the app (brumation plan: “Delete the plan”; geckos: “Delete gecko” or individual entries with ✕; quarantine: “End quarantine”) or uninstall the app. You remove your own photos under “More → Your own photos” with “Original”. When you uninstall the app, Android deletes all of its local data. Saved PDF files and backup files remain until you delete them yourself.

8. Legal basis

To the extent that the controller processes any data at all, this is done to provide the app features you use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Emails you send me are processed on the basis of my legitimate interest in answering your request and improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Since the developer does not store any of your data, you can generally exercise these rights directly in the app. If you have questions, you can reach me at the email address below.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the place where you live.

10. Changes

If the app changes in such a way that other data is processed, this privacy policy will be updated. You can always find the current version in the app under “More”.
