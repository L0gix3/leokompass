Privacy policy for the “Leokompass” app

Last updated: 14 November 2026 (app version 1.6). This is a translation for your convenience; the German version is the legally binding one.

In short: Leokompass stores your brumation plan and your gecko data only on your phone. The developer does not receive any of your data. There is no user account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking services, and the app itself does not load any content from the internet – all texts are built into the app.

1. What data does the app process?

The app only processes data that you enter yourself. It is stored exclusively locally in the protected storage area of the app on your device:

  • Brumation assistant: the date of the fecal sample or of the start, the steps you have ticked off and the checklist
  • optional: “My geckos” – the names of your animals, their sex, and the weights and feedings you enter (feedings only for the last 3 months; older ones are deleted automatically)
  • optional: your own photos that you use instead of the cover photos or for your geckos (see section 2)
  • Settings: light or dark theme, language, animations, expert mode, quiz on or off, feeding interval, whether reminders are switched on, and whether the welcome notice and the beginner question have already been shown
  • Widgets: if you place a Leokompass widget on your home screen, it shows your geckos' names and feeding dates or the brumation status – taken directly from this data on the device

The developer has no access to this data. It is not transmitted to him or to third parties.

2. Your own photos

Under “More → Your own photos” you can replace the cover photos of the home page and the topics with your own photos; under “Geckos” you can give each animal a photo. You choose the photos using the Android photo picker – the app only gets access to the photos you select yourself, not to your other pictures. The app stores a reduced copy of them without location and camera data in the protected storage area of the app. These copies are not transmitted, are not included in the automatic Android/Google backup, and are removed when you reset them (“Original”) or uninstall the app. The original in your gallery remains unchanged.

3. Saving a PDF

When you save the brumation plan as a PDF, you choose the location and file name yourself using the Android file picker. The app only gets access to this one file. Where the file goes from there (e.g. a cloud folder, email, a printer) is up to you; the terms of the respective app or service apply.

4. Links to websites

The app contains links to www.der-leopardgecko.de. If you tap one, your browser opens. Only then – as with any visit to a website – is data such as your IP address transmitted to the operator of that website. That operator's privacy policy applies.

6. Google Play

If you install Leokompass via Google Play, Google processes data in accordance with Google's privacy policy. Through the Google Play Console the developer only receives aggregated statistics (e.g. the number of installations) and – if you have agreed to sharing with developers in your Android settings – anonymous crash reports. The developer cannot identify you from these.

Voluntary donations

Under “More” you can make a voluntary donation to the developer (€1.49, €2.50 or €5.00; the price may differ in your currency). The payment is handled exclusively by Google Play. The developer does not receive any payment details; in the Play Console he only sees the order information that Google provides to sellers (e.g. order number, country, amount). A donation does not unlock any features; the app only learns that the payment was successful so that it can say thank you. Donations go exclusively to the developer of the app, not to the operator of www.der-leopardgecko.de.

7. Storage period and deletion

Your data remains stored until you delete it in the app (brumation plan: “Create a new plan”; geckos: “Delete gecko” or individual entries with ✕) or uninstall the app. You remove your own photos under “More → Your own photos” with “Original”. When you uninstall the app, Android deletes all of its local data. Saved PDF files remain until you delete them yourself.

8. Legal basis

To the extent that the controller processes any data at all, this is done to provide the app features you use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Since the developer does not store any of your data, you can generally exercise these rights directly in the app. If you have questions, you can reach me at the email address below.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the place where you live.

10. Changes

If the app changes in such a way that other data is processed, this privacy policy will be updated. You can always find the current version in the app under “More”.
